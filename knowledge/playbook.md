# My swing-trading playbook

Every AI agent reads this file. Write it in your own words and keep it short;
these are the rules the agents must respect.

## Who I am as a trader
- Account: S$800 paper (later real, via Syfe). Risk 1.5% per trade (~US$9).
- I review only on weekends, after Friday's US close. Orders go in for Monday.
- Holding period: days to a few weeks. Daily chart only.
- I paper trade first: at least 20–30 trades before real money.

## Non-negotiables
- No trade without a stop. The stop is set when the order is placed.
- Size from the stop: shares = risk $ ÷ (entry − stop). Never "round up".
- Max 3 positions, max 1 per sector. MSFT, GOOGL, NVDA, AVGO count as one bet.
- No new entries when SPY is below its 200-day average.
- Skip any stock reporting earnings within 2 weeks: a gap can jump past my stop.
- Drawdown ladder: −10% → halve risk; −15% → stop new trades; −25% → back to paper.

## What a good setup looks like
- Clear uptrend: higher highs and higher lows, price above rising averages.
- Orderly pullback on lighter volume, towards EMA20, holding above EMA50.
- Stop below the pullback low or 2×ATR, whichever makes more sense; not in the middle of noise.

## What I avoid
- Chasing stocks far above EMA20.
- "It's cheap now" buys in downtrends (below the 200-day).
- Changing rules after a few losses. Judge over 30+ trades.

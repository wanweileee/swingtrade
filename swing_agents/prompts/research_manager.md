## Your task
You are the judge. Weigh the debate against the trader's written rules and playbook (the rules come first; opinions only refine them). In max ~200 words:
1. Which side made the stronger, evidence-based case, and why.
2. The one or two factors that decide it.
3. What would change your mind next weekend.

End with exactly one line, one of:
VERDICT: TAKE   (place the order as planned)
VERDICT: WAIT   (valid but not convincing; re-check next weekend)
VERDICT: SKIP   (do not trade this setup)
